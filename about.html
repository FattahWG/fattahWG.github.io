About me

Hi, I'm Fattah.

I'm an SQA Engineer. Most days I test web and mobile applications, write and maintain test automation, and work with developers and business requirements to make sure releases do what they should.

I started in traditional QA, and now I'm growing toward automation-focused, modern quality engineering. On the side, I lead Roblox game projects at Lawak Studio, where I learn game design, teamwork, and product thinking.
